Ads and data

Some Android apps show ads from Google AdMob and ask for consent through Google's consent form where the law requires it. Authorised ad sellers are listed in app-ads.txt. Each app's privacy policy says exactly what it collects. This website counts page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.
