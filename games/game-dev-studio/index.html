Game Dev Studio

Make hit games, beat rival studios and grow from a garage to a campus.

Get it on Google Play Source on GitHub

Start in a garage in 1985 with a single computer and a big dream. Forty years later, will your studio be a legend? Pick a topic and a genre, choose a platform, guide your team through development and wait for the reviews to roll in. Great games win fans and money; flops can sink you.

Features

  • 24 topics, 7 genres and 18 fictional platforms that rise and fall over four decades.
  • Hire and train a team, research better engines and move from a garage to a campus.
  • Build hype, book a booth at the yearly GameExpo and push sales after launch.
  • Yearly trends and rival studios in the industry news.
  • Charts for every game: what it cost, what it earned and when it paid for itself.

Support and privacy

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.
